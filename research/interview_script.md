# Interview script — WhatsApp voice notes tonight

**Rules (Abhay's Mom Test framing):**
- Never pitch. Never mention what I am building.
- Ask about the LAST TIME something happened. Not what they *would* do — what they *did*.
- Get specifics: where, when, what phone, what app, who else was there.
- Emotional temperature counts more than the fix itself.
- Stop after 8–10 minutes. Two conversations, not one.

**Who to call (pick 2, ideally different profiles):**
- Someone who lives in a Tier-2 town or travels a lot (higher outage exposure).
- Someone who is the only earner / handles the family's payments (higher stakes).
- Avoid: MICA classmates (they'll try to be helpful; you want normal users).

---

## Opener (30 sec)

> "Hey — quick favour, I'm doing some research for class. Not selling anything, just want to hear a story from you. Can I get 10 minutes on voice note? No pressure to be polished."

## Q1 — anchor to a real moment (2 min)

> "Think about the last time your phone or SIM stopped working when you actually needed it — like you couldn't call or couldn't get internet. Doesn't have to be dramatic. **When was the last time that happened, and what were you trying to do?**"

*Follow-ups only if they stall:*
- "What phone?"
- "Where were you?"
- "Who was with you?"

## Q2 — the payment moment (3 min)

> "Walk me through what happened when you had to pay someone that day. Or the next day. **What did you actually end up doing — step by step?**"

*Listen for:*
- Did they borrow someone's hotspot? Whose?
- Did they ask a stranger to pay? A friend? A shopkeeper's UPI on trust?
- Did they have cash? How much?
- Did they abandon the transaction?
- Did they walk away and come back later?

## Q3 — the emotional layer (2 min)

> "How did that feel in the moment? Like — were you embarrassed, angry, scared? **What was going through your head?**"

*This is the Mom Test payoff. Do not paraphrase or summarize back. Just let them talk.*

## Q4 — non-consumption probe (1 min)

> "Do you keep any cash on you these days? **When was the last time you actually used it?**"

*If they say "I never carry cash" — that's non-consumption. Note it.*

## Q5 — the workaround map (2 min)

> "Some people I've talked to said they've done things like — carry a second phone, or keep a backup SIM, or a Paytm wallet loaded. **Have you ever done anything like that? Or has anyone in your family?**"

*The workaround IS the signal. If they laugh and say "arre yaar my mom keeps ₹500 hidden in a drawer" — that's your quote.*

## Q6 — close (30 sec)

> "One last thing — how often do you think this kind of thing happens to people you know? Like, a rough guess."

> "Thanks. Sending you food/coffee, tell me your UPI 😄" *(joke — earns you the emotional close, and the irony writes itself)*
