## What to look for when you screenshot

- **Spikes** on the outage queries around known outage dates (Jul 2024 Jio, any Airtel outages) — proves the "national payment event" claim.
- **Persistent baseline** on "UPI not working" — this isn't a one-time thing, it's ambient.
- **Flat / low** "UPI Lite" line — awareness of the fix is negligible compared to the pain, which is the killer chart for "why now: the regulator agrees but adoption hasn't landed."
